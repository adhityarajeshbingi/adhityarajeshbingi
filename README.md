## 💼 Work

### Sambin Technologies · Co-founder and Data & Analytics Lead
*April 2024 to May 2026 · Visakhapatnam, India*

Sambin built **AmyR (Automate My Retail)**, an AI-enabled POS and inventory management platform for small retailers.

- Built ETL workflows that converted **400,000+ raw FMCG product records** into a curated product database of **80,000+ items**, cutting data acquisition costs by **84%**
- Applied NLP to **50,000+ app store reviews** of competing retail apps to identify customer pain points, reducing market research time by **67%**
- Evaluated machine learning approaches for **voice-based product search**, reaching over **80% accuracy**
- Supported **multilingual support across six Indian languages**
- Designed the database schemas and data models behind the product catalogue, inventory and search
- Managed three cross-functional intern teams across data, product design and app development
